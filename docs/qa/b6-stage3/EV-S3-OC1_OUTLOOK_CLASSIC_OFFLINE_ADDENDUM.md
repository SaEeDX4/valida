## 3. Measurements

### 3.1 Method

Tool: [`tools/measure_ev_s3_oc1.py`](tools/measure_ev_s3_oc1.py) (Python 3.13.17, Pillow 12.3.0), run on the preserved originals only. Output: [`measurements.json.txt`](evidence/EV-S3-OC1-outlook-classic-offline/measurements.json.txt), SHA-256 `8176f00afc260dd3472422cbef3c90c1f20bf0f3eebb0ad9d279b63d5b2aed01` (regenerated in this package after two checks were added to the script; the measured values are unchanged).

- **Background** of a text line: the most frequent colour in that line's box inside the 600 px column.
- **Text lines:** rows with any pixel differing from the background by more than 24 in any channel, grouped into lines.
- **Text colour:** the ink pixel with the highest WCAG 2.x contrast against the background ("peak"); the 90th percentile of ink-pixel contrast ("p90") is reported beside it.
- **Contrast:** WCAG 2.x relative luminance, sRGB.
- **Layout:** runs of one colour (≥ 40 px, tolerance 6) along rows y = 300, 650, 700 and 950, and exact-colour runs (≥ 30 px, tolerance 0) along y = 650, locate the column, the canvas and any region outside it.
- **S3:** located in the full screenshots by exact pixel comparison (`S3_crop_of`).
- **Sensitivity:** every light-rendering ratio recomputed with each channel moved by one level (`sensitivity_light_pairs`).
- **Scale:** the logo's black field measured 158 × 36 px in all six full-window screenshots (S1–S2, S4–S7) and in S3.

### 3.2 Reliability and limits

| Factor | Effect here |
| --- | --- |
| Image scaling | **None found.** The 158 × 36 logo is 158 × 36 px, so 1 CSS px = 1 screenshot px; no resampling blurs the text. |
| Compression | None: lossless PNG. |
| Anti-aliasing | Text stroke cores are solid: for **every** text line, peak and p90 are equal, and the same source colour gave the same ink colour in every line and screenshot. Edge pixels lie between ink and background and are excluded or rank below the peak. The measured ratios are therefore those of the solid text colour, which is what WCAG compares. Thin 12 px strokes may *look* fainter than their colour ratio; that is not quantified. |
| Colour transformation | The values are **after** Outlook's transformation — that is the point: they are what the reader saw. The dark renderings equal the template's colours exactly; the light renderings do not. |
| Sensitivity | ±1 level per channel moves the ratios near the thresholds by at most ±0.12 (2.48 → 2.42–2.53; 3.75 → 3.67–3.84; 4.75 → 4.64–4.87; 4.88 → 4.76–5.00) and the headline's 11.14 by ±0.26. No pass/fail result changes. |
| Not covered | Monitor colour management (screenshots are taken before it); other Outlook builds; a light Office theme; other Windows ClearType or contrast settings; zoom other than an effective 100 %. |

## 9. Measurements

### 9.1 Method and reliability

Tool: [`tools/measure_ev_s3_oc2.py`](tools/measure_ev_s3_oc2.py) — the EV-S3-OC1 method (§3.1) with two additions. Output: [`measurements.json.txt`](evidence/EV-S3-OC2-outlook-classic-c2p04-mockups/measurements.json.txt), SHA-256 `2b835af633ec453d3e5f0e4541e944e2fe86307c76cb02515c756048775ebbcb`.

- **These screenshots arrived resized.** The 600 CSS px column measures 479 px (scale **0.798**), against 1.0 in EV-S3-OC1. Where the resizing happened is not known here. Resampling blends thin text strokes with the background, so a line's peak colour may fall short of the real text colour.
- **Classification per line.** "Exact": the peak equals the line's known ink colour — the source colour in the dark rendering, or the light rendering already measured at 1:1 in EV-S3-OC1 (`#F4F7FA` → `#2C3034`, `#B6C0CC` → `#5D6875`). "Blended": it does not; the peak is then a **lower bound**.
- **Lower-bound check.** A resampler can overshoot (produce pixels beyond the ink colour), which would invalidate a lower bound. For every line with a known ink colour, the script counts pixels with more contrast than that ink: **0 in every line** of all six screenshots. No overshoot was found.
- **Masthead:** the band region compared pixel for pixel between the light and dark screenshot of the same message.
- **Not inferred:** exact zoom, network state, and any value not listed below.
